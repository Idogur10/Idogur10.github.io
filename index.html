Who I Am

Research-Driven Engineer

I'm a deep learning engineer completing my M.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering at Ben Gurion University, working in the Biomedical Robotics Lab under Professors Ilana Nisky and Nir Shlezinger.

My research applies deep neural networks to teleoperated robotic surgery — using 3D kinematic data to predict and optimize surgeon hand movements in real time. The resulting model outperforms standard baselines.

Beyond my thesis, I have led multidisciplinary projects in brain-computer interfaces, computer vision, haptic feedback systems, and EEG signal processing, combining rigorous engineering with real-world clinical impact.
